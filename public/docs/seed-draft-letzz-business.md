# LETZZ Business Overview

Partner institutional brief for EOB Capital LETZZ product family.

## Thesis

EOB Capital is a regulated deep-tech development hub. LETZZ Wallet and LETZZ Platform are the reference commercial path for consent-led KYC and AML evidence exchange. The wallet is a reference sample, not the whole company definition.

## Customers

- Primary: natural person (and corporate-linked individuals)
- Secondary: obliged entity staff operating under role tiers

## Revenue design

Fixed and predictable fees. First sale frictionless. Upsells after close. Euro prices unset; model around X = cost per user. OE base access after terms; optional premium reliance only when licensed. Signing free at launch; paid stream contemplated later.

## Integrity

No AML6, eIDAS/EUDI, or ILNAS/QTSP certification claims before Trusted List entry. Hash-first document model. Distinct states: delivery, consent, grant, verification, clearance. Professional language for institutional audiences.
